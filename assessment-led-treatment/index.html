The YK approach

One size has never fit all

"We are known for creating bespoke treatments individually tailored to better suit our clients on the day of their visit."

Most clinics ask you to choose a treatment before anyone has looked at your body. But how you feel today is not how you felt last week, and the pain you can feel is not always where the problem starts.

An assessment-led treatment flips that around. Your therapist listens, assesses how you are moving and where you are holding tension, then draws on whichever techniques fit, deep tissue, sports, myofascial, cupping or osteopathic work, to give you the right treatment for that day. No menus to decode. No guessing.
