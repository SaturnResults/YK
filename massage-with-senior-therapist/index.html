Who This Is For

When you need more than a standard session

There are situations where the depth of clinical experience genuinely matters. Chronic conditions that have resisted previous treatment. Complex presentations involving multiple areas of the body. Long-standing postural problems built up over years. Cases where a detailed assessment needs to happen alongside the hands-on work.

Our senior therapists Yury and Giuseppe bring advanced qualifications, years of clinical practice, and training across multiple modalities. They work with the full toolkit: deep tissue, myofascial release, trigger point therapy, cupping, and more. They are also better placed to identify when a condition needs a different approach entirely and advise you accordingly.

Booking a senior therapist session is also a natural choice if you simply want the very best. There is no medical need required. Many of our regular clients book exclusively with Yury or Giuseppe because they have found the results speak for themselves.

Your Session

What to expect

01

Thorough Consultation

Your therapist takes time at the start of every session to understand your history, presenting issues, previous treatments, and goals. Senior therapists ask the questions others miss.

02

Multi-Modality Treatment

Rather than a single technique, your senior therapist selects and blends the most appropriate tools: deep tissue, myofascial release, trigger point work, cupping and stretching, within a single session.

03

Honest Assessment

You will leave with a clear picture of what was found, what was treated, and what the recommended next steps are, including whether a different type of therapy might be more appropriate.
