The Treatment

What anti-cellulite massage actually does

Cellulite forms when fat deposits push through the connective tissue beneath the skin, creating that characteristic dimpled appearance. It affects the vast majority of women and has nothing to do with weight. It is largely structural, and no amount of diet or exercise alone will fully address it.

Anti-cellulite massage works by combining two complementary techniques. Manual lymphatic drainage stimulates the lymphatic system to clear the excess fluid and metabolic waste that accumulate in the tissue and worsen the appearance. Deep tissue and myofascial techniques then work on the connective tissue itself, breaking down fascial adhesions, improving local circulation, and helping to restore a smoother skin surface over time.

Consistency is important. Clients typically see initial improvements after four to six sessions, with optimal results developing over two to three months. Sessions are followed by aftercare guidance covering hydration, movement, and skincare to help you maintain the benefits between appointments.

Technique

A two-phase approach that actually works

Anti-cellulite massage is not a single technique. Our therapists combine two modalities within each session for the most effective result.

01

Manual Lymphatic Drainage

Light, rhythmic strokes that follow the lymphatic pathways just beneath the skin. This phase clears the fluid retention and metabolic waste that contribute to tissue puffiness and exaggerate the appearance of cellulite.

02

Deep Tissue & Myofascial Work

Firmer techniques targeting the connective tissue and fascia beneath the skin. This phase improves local circulation, breaks down adhesions in the tissue, and helps restore a smoother, firmer skin surface over time.

03

Aftercare Guidance

Your therapist will discuss hydration, movement, and targeted skincare steps to help extend the benefits between sessions. The work you do between appointments makes a real difference to your overall results.

Honest note: Anti-cellulite massage improves the appearance of cellulite and the health of the underlying tissue. It does not permanently remove it, and it is not a weight-loss treatment. Results vary between individuals and depend on consistency. Our therapists will give you an honest picture of what to expect at your initial consultation.

FAQ

Common questions

How many sessions will I need?

Most clients notice initial improvements after four to six sessions. For the best overall result, a course of 12–15 sessions over two to three months is recommended, followed by maintenance appointments every four to six weeks.

Is the treatment uncomfortable?

The lymphatic drainage phase is gentle and deeply relaxing. The deep tissue phase uses firmer pressure and can feel intense in areas of congestion, but your therapist will work within your comfort level throughout. Some temporary redness or tenderness is normal and resolves within a few hours.

Will it help me lose weight?

Anti-cellulite massage improves the appearance of the skin and reduces fluid retention. It is not a weight-loss treatment. Clients often feel and look less puffy due to improved lymphatic drainage, but this is different from fat reduction. Think of it as a treatment for skin quality and tissue health, not body composition.

How long do results last?

Results vary, but clients who complete a full course and maintain with regular appointments typically hold their improvements well. Maintenance sessions every four to six weeks, combined with good hydration and regular movement, make a significant difference to how long the results last.

Can I combine this with other treatments?

Yes, and it works particularly well alongside dedicated lymphatic drainage sessions. Your therapist can advise on the best combination and scheduling to maximise your results.
